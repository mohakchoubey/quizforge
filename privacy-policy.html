Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what QuizForge collects, why, and how it's handled. It applies to quizforge.app and its associated Firebase and API services.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and authentication details, handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Uploaded PDFs: the document you upload to generate a quiz, processed temporarily to extract quiz content.
  • Quiz data: generated questions, your answers, scores, and quiz history, stored in Firebase tied to your account.
  • Contact and feedback messages: anything submitted through the contact or feedback forms, handled by Formspree.
  • Basic technical data: standard request metadata (such as IP address) collected by our hosting and security providers for abuse prevention.

How your PDF is used

An uploaded PDF is sent from our secure backend to Groq to generate quiz content. It is not shown to other users, not used to train any AI model, and is not retained longer than needed to generate and store your quiz.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To generate quizzes from your uploaded PDFs.
  • To save your quiz history so you can review past results.
  • To respond to messages sent through contact or feedback forms.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Third-party services

QuizForge relies on the following services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage.
  • Groq — AI-generated quiz content.
  • Formspree — contact and feedback form delivery.
  • Cloudflare — DNS, HTTPS, and abuse protection.

Each provider processes only the data necessary for its function and is bound by its own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data and quiz history at any time by reaching out through the contact page.

Data security

Account access is protected by Firebase Authentication and Firebase Security Rules, which restrict each user to their own data. No API keys used to generate quizzes are ever exposed in frontend code.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change along with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
