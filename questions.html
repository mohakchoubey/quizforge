FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Can't find your answer here? Reach out directly.

What kind of PDFs work best?

Text-based PDFs — lecture notes, textbook chapters, reports, handouts. Scanned image-only PDFs with no selectable text won't generate good questions.

Why is there a 40-page limit?

It keeps quiz generation fast and focused. If your document is longer, split it into sections and generate a quiz per section — that also tends to produce better, more specific questions.

Can I edit the PDF before generating a quiz?

Yes. The editor lets you trim pages or remove sections you don't want questions pulled from before you move to quiz settings.

What question types are supported?

Multiple choice and short answer, individually or mixed. You choose the format and difficulty on the quiz settings step, before generation.

Do I need an account?

Yes — an account is how your uploads, quizzes, and results are kept private to you and saved for later.

What happens to my uploaded PDF?

It's processed to generate your quiz and isn't shared with other users or used to train any model. See the privacy policy for details.

Is QuizForge free?

Yes, creating an account and generating quizzes is free.

Who's behind QuizForge?

It's an independent project — see the about page for more.
