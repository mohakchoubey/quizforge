About

A quiz generator built for a very specific 2am problem

You have a PDF of notes and a test tomorrow. Re-reading isn't testing yourself. QuizForge exists to close that gap.

What it does

You upload a PDF — up to 40 pages — and choose how many questions you want, how hard they should be, and what format to answer in. QuizForge reads the document and generates a quiz from it, then scores your answers and explains each one afterward.

Why it's built this way

Most quiz tools either ask you to write your own questions or generate generic ones about a topic. Neither is fast when you already have the source material in front of you. QuizForge skips straight to the part that's useful: questions grounded in your document, with your account keeping a history of what you've already studied.

Who built it

QuizForge is an independent project by Mohak Choubey, a developer who builds and maintains a small portfolio of web tools. It's built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, backed by Firebase for accounts and Groq for quiz generation.
